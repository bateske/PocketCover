Details
-------

Pocket Cover's procedural renderer is an original implementation.
The reference projects named in README.md inspired design concepts only;
their code and image assets are not bundled.

src/font.js (fonts[2]) contains a printable-ASCII bitmap subset of MinimalFont5x7.ttf by
Kate Garcia (font copyright metadata: KateisAwesome120), extracted from the
user-provided PixelFonts project,
build/manifest-exports/MinimalFont5x7.json.
The author's listing labels the font "100% Free" and states:
"Feel free to use this font."
Source: https://www.dafont.com/minimalfont5x7.font (checked 2026-10-05).
Original font rights remain with the author. Preserve this notice when copying
the library.

Version 2 also includes printable-ASCII bitmap subsets from the user-provided
TomAndJerry14x10.json (font copyright metadata: StePickford) and
MonoBold8x8.json (font copyright metadata: castpixel). These two local exports
do not include explicit redistribution terms; their original rights remain with
their authors. No additional rights to these fonts are granted by this package.
Review their source-font terms before redistributing those font subsets.

Version 19 adds fonts[3] in src/font.js: a printable-ASCII bitmap subset of
Round9x13 by heraldod (font copyright metadata: Herald), from
https://heraldod.itch.io/bitmap-fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org). The glyphs were rasterized
from Round9x13.ttf on the font's native 13 px pixel grid and are never scaled.
This subset is a Modified Version under the OFL: the '&' glyph (code 38) was
redrawn for Pocket Cover, because the original read as 'G' at 1x. It keeps the
font's 13 rows, advance of 10 and rounded corners. Every other glyph is the
unmodified raster. The font data remains under the OFL 1.1 and may not be sold
by itself. The OFL does not cover the rest of this package.
